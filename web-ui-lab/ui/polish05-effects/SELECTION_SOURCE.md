The selection plates in this directory were captured from the approved
`assets/ui/polish05-import/reference/ProjectS_UI_Polish05_Workbench.html` by
`scripts/export_polish05_selection.cjs` at its native 1440×920 browser size.

The browser's selected and unselected gear rows, tab gradients and underline,
inventory slots, refinery recipes, and catalyst states were captured with their
children hidden. The plate pixels include CSS gradients, borders, shadows, and
the small selected-gear diamond. Names, item art, values, and hitboxes are
rendered separately in the isolated Minecraft lab.
