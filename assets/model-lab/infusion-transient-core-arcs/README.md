The Creator selected the 20:10 scene in https://www.bilibili.com/video/BV1Ss411A73D/?t=1210 (Thaumcraft 6 Beta19). The parent research worker personally replayed the sequence and relayed these approximate observations: no arc at 20:13.92; a short bent pink-purple arc near the lower-left of the core at 20:14.06; a slightly outward-bulging bent shape at 20:14.22; the clear arc disappears by 20:14.35. The decimal times are approximate because they were read immediately before screenshots. This worker did not independently replay the video. No original reference footage or sprites are included.

The observation supports a brief birth, a small shape change and disappearance over roughly 0.3 seconds. It does not establish smooth writhing (the camera moves), the exact occluded root, an external fixed connection, or whether it is flux or essence intake. This proposal makes no such identification.

The independent offline sampler produces one short four-node arc for six server ticks (0.3 seconds), with a small outward bend change and diminishing light. It has no permanent line, ring or continuous orbit. The rejected static filament texture is not used. Proposed frequency is one event every 0.9 seconds in idle and every 0.55 seconds in active ritual; event periods exceed the lifetime, so the line vanishes between events.
