# Wave 1 / Lane A — Product and Core Loop

Isolation: the lane read only `CONSTITUTION.md`; it did not inspect the repo or other proposals.

Proposal: **Tidebreak Anchorage — Anchorback blockade**.

The player is a free contractor trapped in a compact port whose trade route is blocked by a sea creature. The loop is:

`learn the port → contribute as hunter, salvager, or outfitter → trade for missing inputs → craft and install a mixed-material breakwater brace → use the repaired structure in the boss fight → turn a personal boss catalyst plus ordinary materials into one weapon MOD`

The port is a two-minute walk containing Ferry Landing, Lantern Quay, Salt Steps, Pitch Grotto, and Broken Breakwater. Each place serves at least two of quest, gathering, combat, trade, traversal learning, or boss preparation.

Five NPC motives were defined: a warden racing the next supply ship, a tidekeeper who knows the ecological cause, a riveter repairing their reputation, a broker profiting from reopening, and a lookout teaching readable boss tells after a past loss.

The player borrows one Boarding Spear with Thrust and Brace. Three proposed post-boss MODs trade precision damage, stagger strength, and wet-terrain mobility. No character level is required.

The first 5 minutes establish the crisis, weapon, contribution choice, and first value-producing action. The first 25–40 minutes complete contribution, trade, brace installation, ordinary-enemy tutorial, boss, catalyst, and MOD. Two-hour replay comes from a different contribution route, MOD, market role, and mastery commission rather than raw level inflation.

Failure preserves quest work and gathered materials. A boss wipe resets only the encounter and allows a retry in under 60 seconds. Personal rewards require valid participation and are granted only on victory.

Primary risks: trader route becoming an NPC errand, fallback prices killing player trade, unclear charge induction, water friction, one dominant MOD, and scope creep beyond a 40-minute first clear.

Key acceptance checks: Vanilla/resource-pack-declined completion; three opening routes within 20% time; multiple motivated NPCs; no finished enemy equipment; two-channel boss tells; no pre-boss regrind after a wipe; server authority; every location has two roles.
