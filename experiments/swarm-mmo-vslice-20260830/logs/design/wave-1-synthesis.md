# Wave 1 Synthesis — Independent Convergence

The three lanes were isolated from each other and from existing ProjectS content. Their convergence was not prompted by shared names or repo details.

## Strong convergence

All three independently selected:

- one compact port blocked by a sea/shore creature;
- a single spear/pike/hook weapon with basic thrust plus timing-based Brace;
- one shell-bearing shoreline enemy family that teaches boss charge/stagger rules;
- ordinary enemies dropping a component, never finished gear;
- two gathered materials from terrain-connected routes;
- one mixed-material repair component that opens or repairs the boss route;
- a bounded market/fallback stock so specialists can buy missing inputs and solo cannot deadlock;
- a boss in a breakwater/drydock using charge lines, raised safe terrain, water/flood pressure, and break/stagger windows;
- a personal boss catalyst that must be crafted with ordinary inputs into an installed weapon MOD;
- resource-pack-optional visuals with Vanilla particle, sound, BossBar, actionbar, and geometry carrying all mechanics;
- a first clear around 20–40 minutes and low-cost encounter reset.

This common core is the leading candidate for the Current Contract.

## Open disagreements for later adjudication

- Whether the standard weapon appearance should be a trident, iron hoe, or another item.
- Whether combat needs Sprint+Attack as a distinct Drive action or only Thrust+Brace.
- Two versus three MOD choices.
- Fixed-price exchange versus bounded stock-sensitive pricing.
- One selected contribution writ versus three stamps that may be satisfied in any sourcing style.
- Whether a repaired pillar is required to expose the boss or Brace alone fills Break.
- Whether the world should be closer to 160×120 blocks or simply constrained by travel-time targets.

## Scope pressure to red-team

The three-lane convergence is coherent but may still be too large. Red-team must challenge dynamic economy, multiplayer reward eligibility, water manipulation, world-state versus personal-state gating, multiple MODs, post-clear mastery commissions, and persistence breadth before the Contract is frozen.
