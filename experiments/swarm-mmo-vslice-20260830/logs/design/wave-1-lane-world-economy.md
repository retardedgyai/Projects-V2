# Wave 1 / Lane B — World, Exploration, and Economic Ecology

Isolation: the lane read only `CONSTITUTION.md`; it did not inspect the repo or other proposals.

Proposal: **Narrowwake Outpost**, approximately 160×120 blocks, focused entirely on reopening a blocked tidal gate.

Spatial graph:

`Tidal Flat ↔ Harbor Market ↔ Quarry Cut`, with a Tide Cave shortcut joining the resource routes to the Breakwater Passage, a Signal Cliff observation route above, and the boss breakwater beyond the gate.

- Harbor Market: spawn, respawn, NPCs, writ, exchange, assembly, and MOD fitting.
- Tidal Flat: low-risk Ropekelp gathering and a hidden cave entrance.
- Quarry Cut: Tideiron, the ordinary enemy, height-based combat, and a shortcut.
- Tide Cave: guaranteed scarce node, boss foreshadowing, and avoidance route.
- Signal Cliff: observation quest and natural teaching of charge lines, pillars, and flood lanes.
- Boss Breakwater: low flooded lane, raised platforms, and three signal pillars used by boss mechanics.

Four NPCs cover harbor responsibility, tide knowledge, crafting/MOD work, and bounded exchange stock.

The Harbor Writ has Security, Supply, and Repair stamps. Any stamp input may be self-produced or purchased. A Signal Coupler consumes combat-derived hinge plus gathered Tideiron and Ropekelp Resin, connecting combat, gathering, crafting, trade, quest, gate opening, and boss preparation. A one-time repair-order profit prevents the craft/trade start from lacking capital while forbidding repeat currency generation.

One Brineclaw enemy family teaches frontal shell, sidestep, charge induction, and weak-joint exposure. One Boarding Pike uses server-authoritative melee plus Brace. One giant Cairnback boss composes Sweep, pillar-directed Charge, and low-lane Rising Wash; its final phase recombines learned rules instead of adding a surprise.

The boss awards a personal Pressure Pearl catalyst, not finished gear. The catalyst, ordinary materials, and Marks upgrade the currently chosen MOD.

Primary risks: reserve stock either erasing professions or causing solo deadlock, repeat-order currency duplication, particle clutter, latency-sensitive water/knockback, unavoidable combat on the gathering route, world-state gates skipping personal quest state, and oversized market simulation.

Key acceptance checks: every location within 60 seconds of market; no scenic dead ends; every stamp obtainable by production or trade; negative buy/sell arbitrage; idempotent first-order reward; gatherer avoidance route; Vanilla-readable boss; persistent writ/MOD/reward state.
